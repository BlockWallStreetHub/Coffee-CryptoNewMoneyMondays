DCA vs lump sum

Lump sum = investing the same total all at once on your start date. Neither is "better" in general: lump sum usually wins when price trends up, DCA smooths the ride and removes the need to pick a day.

Past results do not predict the future. Prices come from Coinbase's BTC-USD daily closes. For other currencies they're converted at each day's official exchange rate (Frankfurter / ECB). Fees and taxes aren't included.
