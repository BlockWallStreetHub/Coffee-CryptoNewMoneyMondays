Yield Farm Trading

We're providing liquidity on Uniswap with WBTC / WETH, so the Bitcoin and Ethereum stack earns trading fees while the market moves. This page reads both positions straight from the blockchain, live.

How to read this

In range = earning

A liquidity position only earns fees while the price sits inside its range. The green zone on each bar is the range. The white marker is where price is right now.

Unclaimed fees

Trading fees collect inside the position until they're claimed. This is what the position has earned and not yet withdrawn.

Out of range = paused

If price leaves the range, the position stops earning and sits fully in one of the two coins. That's the signal to rebalance, wait, or collect.
